How to Play

  • Move: ← → or A / D  ·  Jump: Space / W / ↑ (press twice to double jump)
  • On a phone: drag the joystick at the bottom-left of the game screen to move, tap the big JUMP button at the bottom-right (tap again mid-air to double jump). Tap ⛶ for fullscreen.
  • Collect all 36 gold coins & cigars — each one is worth +$100.
  • Stomp FUD rats and Paper Hands to put them down.
  • Boss: The Bear guards the helicopter. Stomp him 5 times to put him down.
  • At the helicopter: press S to SELL and end the run with your balance, or H to HOLD — fly to the moon, climb the mountain of gold, grab the 100B MC flag on top, and your balance becomes $1,000,000.
  • Don't fall into the pits. You have 3 lives.
